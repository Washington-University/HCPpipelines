The cifti files in this folder are not the standard Grayordinate spaces, they contain only the left and right (cerebral) cortical surface indices, with no subcortical voxels.  These are not generally recommended, but are used for some surface-specific measures such as curvature or sulcal depth.
For most other data, please use the standard Grayordinate spaces instead:
For 32k, please refer to /global/templates/91282_Greyordinates
For 59k, please refer to /global/templates/170494_Greyordinates
For 79k, please refer to /global/templates/231440_Greyordinates
